This Privacy Policy describes how SCKH (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our Amazon advertising tools and related services that use Login with Amazon and the Amazon Ads API.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may process:

  • Account identifiers provided through Login with Amazon (such as an Amazon user identifier and associated advertising profile identifiers).
  • Advertising account and campaign data accessed through the Amazon Ads API after you authorize access (for example campaign settings, performance metrics, and related advertising metadata).

2. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Authenticate you and obtain authorization to call the Amazon Ads API on your behalf.
  • Create, manage, analyze, and optimize Amazon advertising campaigns as you request.

  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable law and Amazon’s API terms.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

4. Data retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of data we control, subject to legal and operational requirements.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may revoke our access to your Amazon advertising data at any time through your Amazon account settings or by contacting us. Revoking access may limit or stop the related features of our service.

7. Children’s privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
